What is SoundLab Release Service?

SoundLab Release Service helps independent Nigerian and African artists put their music on every major platform: Spotify, Apple Music, Audiomack, Boomplay, TikTok, YouTube Music and more.

You send your track, artist name and release date on WhatsApp. SoundLab prepares the metadata, cover art, promo clips and a release-day plan, depending on your package, and you approve everything before it goes out.

It is run by Ziv Williams, who releases music himself as WYDERZIV.

Highlights

  • Spotify, Apple Music, Audiomack, Boomplay
  • TikTok and YouTube Music
  • Metadata, cover art and promo clips
  • Release-day plan
  • You keep your royalties and masters
