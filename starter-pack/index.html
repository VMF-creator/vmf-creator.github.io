What is Lagos Small Business Starter Pack?

The Lagos Small Business Starter Pack is a ready-made design kit for Nigerian small businesses: food spots, salons, barbers, fashion, events, shops and services.

It has 30 editable flyers, menus and price lists, 85 WhatsApp status and post captions, and 12 customer reply scripts. You edit everything on your phone in Canva or PowerPoint, add your prices and logo, and post the same day.

There is a do-it-yourself pack and a version where it is set up for you.
