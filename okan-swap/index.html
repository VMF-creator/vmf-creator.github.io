What is OKAN Swap?

OKAN Swap is a simple web page for swapping Solana tokens. Connect your own wallet, pick the tokens, and it finds the best route across Solana exchanges.

It is non-custodial: OKAN Swap never holds your coins or your keys, and every swap is signed in your own wallet.

It is built by SolutionB20 in Lagos. Crypto prices move fast; only swap what you can afford to lose.

Highlights

  • Any Solana token
  • Best route across exchanges
  • Non-custodial: your wallet, your keys
  • Works in the phone browser with a wallet app
