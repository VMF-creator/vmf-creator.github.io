What is SolutionB20?

SolutionB20 is a digital agency in Lagos, Nigeria, founded by Ziv Williams. It builds systems that bring in the right clients, filter them, and turn them into paying customers.

That means lead systems that send you a steady flow of qualified prospects, calls that book themselves with automatic follow-up so no lead is lost to a slow reply, one clear pipeline for every client and deal, and mobile websites built to convert, not just to look nice. The SolutionB20 Academy teaches these skills to beginners.

Every project starts with a free audit: how leads find you, how you follow up, and where deals slip away.
