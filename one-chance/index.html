What is One Chance?

One Chance is a free 3D open-street game set in Lagos, Nigeria. It runs straight in your phone's web browser, so there is nothing to install and no app store. It also works offline once it has loaded, and there is an Android app.

You start with one chance and hustle your way across the city: from Ojota to Lekki, through Yaba, Ikeja and the Island. Catch a danfo or a keke, hop on an okada, drive your own car, take the chopper, or board a flight from Eko International Airport.

The city is full of life: street crowds, shops, a barber, Eko Mercy Specialist Hospital, your own crib with a sofa and a kitchen, a soccer pitch, police on patrol, day and night, and a map of Lagos local government areas. Watch out for one chance buses.
