What is Lagos Stays?

Lagos Stays is a simple way to book a shortlet apartment or hotel in Lagos through WhatsApp, with places that have been checked first.

It covers Lekki, Victoria Island, Ikoyi, Ikeja, Yaba and Ajah. Pick the area, dates, guests and budget, and it opens WhatsApp with everything filled in. Before a place is recommended, the host, the address and the photos are checked against the real apartment.

Hosts and hotels can list for free and pay a 10% commission only when a booking is confirmed.
