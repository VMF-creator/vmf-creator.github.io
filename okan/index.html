What is OKAN?

OKAN (Ọkàn means "heart" in Yoruba) is a personal assistant app built in Lagos, Nigeria. You can talk to it by voice or by text in Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, French and English, and it answers in your language.

OKAN helps with everyday work: answering questions, reading documents, writing CVs, business plans and messages, finding real business leads with checked phone numbers and emails, creating images, building simple bots and agents, and planning your day.

It opens in any phone browser, can be added to your home screen like an app, and there is an Android app. Sign in with Google or email to keep your chats and history.
